# Global Community Standards & Code of Conduct

**Effective Date:** November 27, 2026 · **Version:** v2.0 · **Document ID:** `DOC-06`  
**Organization:** The Lunar Org · **Web Reader:** [lunarorg.pages.dev/legal/conduct](https://lunarorg.pages.dev/legal/conduct)  
**Jurisdictions Covered:** Global

> Universal behavioral standards, anti-harassment policy, and enforcement procedures across all countries and community spaces.

---

## 1. Universal Scope & Inclusivity

This Code of Conduct applies globally to all community members, contributors, users, and maintainers across all Lunar Org spaces—including Discord servers, issue trackers, email support, and public discussions—regardless of country, nationality, race, ethnicity, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, religion, or technical experience level.

## 2. Expected and Prohibited Behavior

Participants are expected to communicate constructively, respect differing perspectives, and prioritize technical clarity. The following behaviors are strictly prohibited:

- Harassment, intimidation, hate speech, discriminatory slurs, or personal attacks in public or private channels.
- Doxxing or publishing another person's private or identifying information without explicit consent.
- Unwelcome sexualized language, imagery, or advances.
- Spam, impersonation, trolling, or deliberate disruption of support and engineering workflows.

## 3. Reporting, Enforcement, and Proportionality

Violations may be reported confidentially to contact.theraj01@gmail.com or via official moderation channels. Maintainers review all reports promptly and impartially and may issue warnings, temporary mutes, or permanent bans depending on severity, while preserving user privacy.
