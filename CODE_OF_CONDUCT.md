# Community Guidelines

The Lunar Org is a space for thoughtful collaboration, experimentation, and quiet craftsmanship.

### Principles

- **Respect & Kindness:** Treat contributors and members with patience, clarity, and constructive feedback.
- **Thoughtful Dialogue:** Welcome diverse backgrounds and technical perspectives. Keep discussions focused on building better software.
- **Zero Harassment:** Harassment, toxicity, or exclusionary behavior of any kind is not tolerated.
