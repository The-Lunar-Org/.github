# Trademark and Brand Guidelines

**Effective Date:** November 23, 2026  
**Organization:** The Lunar Org  
**Maintainer / Trademark Proprietor:** Himanshu Raj (@ItsHimanshu-Raj)

---

## 1. Overview and Purpose

These Trademark and Brand Guidelines (the "Guidelines") govern the use of all trademarks, service marks, trade names, logos, wordmarks, domain names, emblems, and trade dress (collectively, the "Marks") owned, controlled, or used by **The Lunar Org** (the "Organization"). 

The purpose of these Guidelines is to protect the integrity, reputation, and goodwill associated with the Organization's brand, while facilitating legitimate community commentary, nominative fair use, and open-source software collaboration.

---

## 2. Ownership of Marks

The Marks include, without limitation:
- The wordmark **"The Lunar Org"** and **"Lunar"** as applied to software development, cloud infrastructure, Discord integrations, automated telemetry tools, and associated digital services.
- The official stylized logo, monogram, emblem, and geometric marks of The Lunar Org.
- All repository titles, package namespaces, organization identifiers (`The-Lunar-Org`), and visual iconography originating from the Organization.

All goodwill generated by any authorized or permitted use of the Marks inures solely and exclusively to the benefit of The Lunar Org.

---

## 3. Permitted Uses (No License Required)

Subject to compliance with these Guidelines, you may use the Marks without prior written permission solely under the following limited circumstances:

### 3.1 Nominative Fair Use
You may use the wordmark "The Lunar Org" or "Lunar" strictly in factual, non-confusing text descriptions to refer directly to the software, repositories, or services provided by the Organization (e.g., *"This plugin integrates with The Lunar Org's telemetry service"* or *"Built for use with lunar-bot"*).

### 3.2 Attribution and Hyperlinking
You may display official badges, unmodified logos, or text links to drive traffic directly to official repositories (`https://github.com/The-Lunar-Org`) or official communication channels (`https://discord.gg/hSvuG9bcFN`).

---

## 4. Prohibited Uses

Unless expressly authorized in writing by the Organization, the following acts are strictly prohibited:

### 4.1 False Affiliation or Endorsement
You may not use the Marks in any manner that implies, suggests, or causes reasonable confusion that your project, website, product, company, or service is officially sponsored, endorsed, authored, certified, or maintained by The Lunar Org.

### 4.2 Deceptively Similar Branding
You may not adopt, display, register, or use any trademark, service mark, trade name, logo, or icon that is confusingly similar to, derived from, or an imitation of any Mark of The Lunar Org.

### 4.3 Domain Names and Social Identifiers
You may not register, license, or maintain any internet top-level domain name, subdomain, social media handle, Discord server name, or organization moniker containing the word "The Lunar Org", "Lunar Org", or any confusingly similar variation without prior express written consent.

### 4.4 Commercial Exploitation
You may not sell, license, merchandise, or commercially distribute physical or digital goods (including clothing, hardware, non-fungible tokens, or paid software distributions) bearing the Marks without a formal, written commercial trademark license executed by the Organization.

### 4.5 Modification and Distortion
You may not distort, re-color, stretch, alter the proportions of, crop, or animate the official logos or emblems of The Lunar Org. The Marks must always be displayed in their authorized forms with appropriate clearance margins.

---

## 5. Proper Trademark Attribution

Whenever a Mark is referenced in documentation, publication, or commercial contexts where permitted, the following attribution statement must appear prominently:

> *"The Lunar Org, Lunar, the Lunar logo, and associated brand identifiers are trademarks of The Lunar Org. All rights reserved."*

The Lunar Org reserves all legal and equitable rights to monitor the use of its Marks and to revoke permission, issue cease-and-desist notices, or initiate formal legal proceedings against any party that infringes upon, dilutes, or misappropriates the Marks.
