## 2. Permitted Nominative Fair Use

Without prior written consent, third parties may reference our wordmarks strictly in truthful, non-misleading, factual prose to identify our products (for example, compatibility statements, editorial reviews, security advisories, or academic citations), provided that:

- The reference uses only the wordmark and does not display our stylized logos or trade dress as your own branding.
- The context makes clear that your project, publication, or service is independent and not endorsed, certified, or sponsored by The Lunar Org.
- Proper attribution is included where practicable: "The Lunar Org, LunarQuest, LunarOffice, and associated logos are trademarks of The Lunar Org."

## 3. Strictly Prohibited Uses

Without an express written license executed by The Lunar Org, you may not:
